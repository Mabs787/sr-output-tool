Help & support

Service

  1. On this page, the services available for your package will be displayed. Some services have additional options, tracking, or requiring a signature. Some tracked services also allow SMS and email notifications. These will be sent by Royal Mail or Parcelforce to the phone number and/or email address provided to let you know when your item is on its way. You will only see the services which are available for the item you’re sending.

    • Some Parcelforce services have extended delivery times for certain zones/postcodes. Find out more here.

  2. Delivery aims shown for each service are based on the majority of UK addresses. However, delivery times may vary depending on the destination postcode. For services where delivery is stated as an aim rather than a guarantee, no delivery target time is provided for items sent to the following postcode areas:

    • AB30–38, AB41-45, AB51–56, AB99, BT, DD8-10, DG7-9, FK8, FK15–21, HS, IV, KA27–28, KW1–17, PA20–38, PA41–80, PH10-11, PH13-14, PH 17-18, PH19–44, PH49–50, TD15, TR21–25, GY, IM, JE, ZE.

    In these cases, delivery may take longer and no estimated delivery timeframe is guaranteed.

  3. Depending on the services selected, item cover may also be offered to provide compensation for damage or part loss. Select the cover amount closest to the value of your items.

  4. Click the ‘Continue’ button.

Destination

  1. On this page, please enter the address where you want to send your item. If you're sending within the United Kingdom, you can begin typing part of the address or postcode into the Postcode search field, or alternatively, click the ‘Enter address manually’ link to show all available fields. As a minimum, you must include a name or company name, the first line of the address, and a town/city. For items within the United Kingdom, a postcode is also required.
  2. If you’re sending items outside of the United Kingdom, you’ll be asked to include further details about what your package contains in this section. Depending on the country you have selected, these extra details may be mandatory for customs.
    • The customs declaration category is required to describe the basis on which the goods are being sent.
    • The item description should be a short description of the item you're sending. For example, 'books', 'photographs', 'clothes' or 'chocolate'.
    • The quantity refers to the number of this type of item that you're sending. For example, if you're sending 2 books, enter '2' here.
    • The item weight and item value should be the weight (in grams or kilograms) and value (in pounds) of a single one of these items. We multiply these values by the quantity to get the total weight, which is displayed underneath each of these fields.
    • If you're sending more than one type of item in a single package, use the 'Add another item' button further down the page to describe the other items that you're sending.
    • If your destination country is the USA, a customs fee (including all duties and taxes) and a handling fee will be calculated after you have entered your package contents. This is mandatory when sending items to the USA and is based on the contents you declare. This will also show in your basket with the postage, and on your purchase confirmation, and will be charged in addition to postage. Find out more about customs and handling fees.
    • If your destination country is France, any applicable customs charges (including duties and taxes) and a handling fee will be calculated after you enter your package contents. These charges will be shown in your basket and on your purchase confirmation and are payable in addition to postage. You can choose to pay these charges at the time of purchase, or allow the recipient to pay them on delivery.

Sending parcels with a Postbox

If you’re sending a letter or small parcel with us, simply follow the steps below to find and drop off your items at a postbox.

  1. Buy postage online (you can do this online or using our app) or purchase a stamp for your letter
  2. Print your label and pop it on or affix your stamp and write out your address
  3. Use our app to scan your label for proof of posting
  4. Find your nearest postbox, post your item and leave the rest to us
